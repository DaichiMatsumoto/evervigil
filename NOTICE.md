## Original icon artwork

The EverVigil icon is original project artwork generated by the repository's
deterministic icon script. It consists only of neutral geometric
shapes, contains no text, letters, glasses, lenses, paired forms, certification
marks, or recognition badges, and was created independently.

It is not derived from, traced from, or based on any Even Realities artwork.

The replaceable assets are centralized at:

- `src/EverVigil/Assets/evervigil-placeholder-source.png` — 1024 x 1024 source.
- `src/EverVigil/Assets/evervigil-placeholder.ico` — 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48,
  64, 128, and 256 pixel images.

Application, tray, installer, uninstaller, and About surfaces use this single
asset family. It is the project's adopted icon and is not a claim of
certification, endorsement, or affiliation.
